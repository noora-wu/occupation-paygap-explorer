Guide

How to use this bubble chart

1. Read the position

The vertical 0% line means equal average hourly wages. Bubbles to the right show occupations where men earn more on average; bubbles to the left show occupations where women earn more on average.

2. Compare size and height

Higher bubbles represent occupations with a larger share of the workforce. Larger bubbles also mean more workers, so the biggest bubbles are the most common jobs in that year.

3. Use colour for occupation change

Green means the occupation's workforce share has grown since 1981. Red means its workforce share has declined since 1981.

4. Explore with the controls

Use the timeline or Play years button to watch change over time. Filter by gap size, search for a specific occupation, hover for quick details, and click a bubble to keep that occupation selected across years.
